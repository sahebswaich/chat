# II. Origins — the coin, the fund and the first exchange (2021 → 2025)

Between March 2021 and October 2025 Saheb wrote dozens of crypto documents: plans, specs, term sheets, trade letters, spreadsheets and one-line notes. Read in order, they hold three ideas sketched again and again: **a main coin that owns many product coins**, **an options-led fund that wants to become a prime bank**, and **a leveraged exchange where the house coin earns the fees**. This chapter sorts every idea from the period into eight pods and ends with a dated timeline.

## The short version {#origins-summary}

1. **The coin.** In August 2022 one currency (OHM) prices everything in the "World of Ohm". By October 2022 it is a fixed-supply main token above a family of app tokens, paying dividends in USDC. In 2023 Saheb writes the rules down ("the ecosystem's coins must be less than the main coin") and, in *Coin Denominated Reality*, describes a public company whose main coin holds 100+ revenue-sharing product coins. This is the seed of the unified "Prime" coin.
2. **The fund.** Andromeda runs from a $20m tokenized partnership through pooled ETH and SOL option trades to a $25m offshore fund brochure (3S Capital). Its stated end point: a market-making firm that grows "until we're the first legal crypto Prime bank in the world."
3. **The first exchange.** The venue is sketched at least six times before GLX, from an OHM forex exchange with "Up to 500x leverage" (2022) and a BTCZPERP pair on the Z stablecoin (2023) to the "Andromeda Terminal" for multi-exchange perps (2024).

> [!number] $1.3M — raised by Manifest "in 8 hours" (2021–2022)
> The only early project with outside money the documents confirm. It sunsetted.

> [!number] 100m × $400 = $40b — Saheb's main-coin rule of thumb (Aug 2023)
> "If you have 100m tokens at 40, and they go to 400, you have 40b."

## Manifest: redeemable goods and the World of Ohm (2021 → 2023) {#origins-manifest}

Manifest is the first project Saheb ran, the only one with confirmed outside money, and the brand he later put on his operating system. It began as a token-backed streetwear collective and ended as a plan to sell itself to Olympus DAO.

### MNFST and the genesis hoodie (late 2021)

The FAQ (2021-12-19) calls Manifest "a decentralized streetwear collective, democratizing ownership of business". MNFST was a rebasing token in the OHM-fork style (a rebasing token raises every holder's balance on a schedule). NFTs sold for MNFST. Bonds took in WETH, sOHM and MNFST-OHM LP, so the protocol owned its liquidity: "we earn the swap fees because we own all our liquidity".

- The genesis hoodie NFT sale (ERC-1155) was the token sale: "Every genesis hoodie NFT that was minted automatically staked 33 MNFST in the minter's wallet."
- The treasury earned daily income on OHM and KLIMA.
- Three verticals: **Pipeline** (a physical good paired with an on-chain proof of ownership), **Streetwear** (aimed at the "185 billion dollar streetwear industry") and **Mafia** (treasury growth through DeFi yield, partnerships and acquisitions).

The résumé (2025-11-12) dates Manifest October 2021 – July 2022: "Raised $1.3M and partnered with a factory in Los Angeles to produce redeemable NFT-linked products". The yami proposal (2024-01-11) says "raised 1.3m in 8 hours" and that the project "sunsetted".

> [!tension] The 2026 GLX investor document says "I built an algostable at seventeen, in forty days, and raised a million dollars at it in eight hours." The 8-hour raise matches Manifest, but in April 2021, before Manifest, Saheb called himself "a like minded 18 year old".

### The pitch deck (April 2022)

The deck (manifest.gg, 2022-04-29) pitches "Digital Provenance of physical goods" in three lines: **Manifest Bridge**, open tooling that links a frontend to a Shopify inventory to mint redeemable NFTs; **MaaS (Manifest-as-a-Service)**, "Design to Doorstep" merch for crypto communities through Los Angeles Fashion District factories, scalable to "a project every two weeks", redeemed at manifest.gg/inventory; and a **luxury streetwear brand** for "the crypto nouveau rich". Its tagline: "THEY WANT YOU TO OWN NOTHING, WE WANT YOU TO OWN EVERYTHING."

> [!quote] "until we actually launch our own token (not until there is regulatory clarity in the United States) *we learned our lesson launching a dao and token to early its not a good thing, it's a huge distraction that prevents focus on building." — Manifest Latest_Pitch_Deck, Apr 2022

The deck holds the first written trace of the exchange: "I've always wanted to trade options on miami condos, perpetuals on sneakers, japanese whiskey futures and long all these items and financialize these real world items." It names Manifold.xyz, RTFKT and Metafactory among competitors, and lists unlabeled names (RHINO, MILARK, THEHEYYMAN/GSV, R3LIC, ALTAMIRA, KRILL, DALA), probably collaborators.

### The Olympus plan and the "World of Ohm" (August 2022)

On 2022-08-18 Saheb wrote: "Olympus acquires Manifest for 33m and me. Accept anything above 25m", and "I operator at Olympus for 25m". First he would buy out Milark's stake, pay out Dala and clear Manifest's debts. The plan's pieces, all priced in OHM:

- **Forex.** "Olympus creates first decentralized forex trading exchange. Pairs OHM against currencies, synthetics. Purely frontend, not actually coins." OHM/USD, OHM/EUR and OHM/JPY, "Up to 500x leverage", positions denominated in OHM, lower fees for OHM holders, revenue to OHM holders. Lazer Devshop builds it, with referral links and a leaderboard.
- **Options** on gOHM, BTC and ETH: "don't give out any APIs on how to market make because we'll install our own market making team" (the team is called "Gemini"). Revenue: fees, "being the casino", referrals and spreads.
- **Flow.** Flow runs clothing drops in OHM through sudoswap-style pools ("stockx like markets") and pays quarterly stablecoin dividends; Olympus would own 16–17%. "CMill builds out Flow, with the 25m gnosis locked with me him and olympus officials, it's my personal piggybank."
- **A Cash-App-style super-app** ("Cash App x Rainbow"): debit card (buy Mover), credit score (buy ArcX), borrowing, clothing, a TikTok-like creator app and the "Oddessy" marketplace, in five tabs.
- **Carbon**: "bailout and buyout KLIMA, kill their token, keep the product allow people to carbon offset in OHM."

The outcome is not recorded.

Shared documents place Saheb in the OHM-fork scene: the **PhantomDAO <> Olympus Proposal** (2021-12-02; a Fantom fork run as a treasury-backed accelerator), the **Concave Master Fork List** (2021-12-05), the **OhmieDay Drop FAQ** (2022-03-05) and **Mecasso** (2021-11-10), NFT streetwear with swappable patches, a near-twin of Manifest Streetwear. He is named in none of them, and the deck's "launching a dao and token to early" does not say which DAO it means.

## The main coin and its product coins: the seed of Prime (2022 → 2025) {#origins-main-coin}

This is the most consistent design in the early work. For four years Saheb returns to one structure: **a scarce main coin at the top, many cheaper product coins below it, revenue flowing up as USDC dividends, and one identity across every app.** Each version adds a rule. Together they are the fullest description of a "Prime" coin written before 2026.

### Version 1: one denomination (August 2022)

In the World of Ohm, OHM prices forex, options, clothing, NFTs, credit, the debit card and carbon; holding it cuts fees and earns exchange revenue. The coin is account, price list and dividend right at once.

### Version 2: the Year-1 token stack (October 2022)

*Year 1* turns the single currency into a family. Its first line is "Display All values in USDC/Our Token".

- **Tokens**: "Main (adtech bidding) / Bank / Gambling / Social Media / User Tag".
- **The main token sells blockspace.** Apps and advertisers "bid usdc for chain space/ads"; the chain splits the USDC "⅓ foundation, ⅓ paid to apps, ⅓ dividends to users, anyone can integrate the adtech to their app".
- **Product tokens**: gambling ("if we raise at 300m, this token could be worth 10-30"); social ("could get to 100m (billions in years...US Tiktok is the goal)"); gaming ("super minor"); the bank gets "no token until markets, lending, credit scores".
- **Supply** (linked *Cloud Based Streamable Operating System* note): "Connected Devices Are The Nodes / Fixed Supply / Stakers are eligible to dividends / 5G allows this to be possible now".
- **Currency becomes security**: "All Tokens Pay Dividend eventually starts as currency -> security. Eventually Tokens can put out corporate bonds to raise debt and have financial sheets, this is basically trad companies on chain."
- **Governance**: "Handle Token owners can vote on the fees to set on the protocol, foundation sets the default to 3%." "Dividend decided by foundation (decided by me, unilaterally @god)". "foundation holds all tokens, equity partners hold tokens, no vest."
- **Listing**: "Get on coinbase/gemini stat."
- **A possible name**: handles are priced in "USDC/REAL at current rates". REAL may be the intended main-token name; the note does not say.

Identity is the **LIFE handle**, bought in USDC at first OS start ("$3 plus change plus tax if kyc"; the change pays gas for 20–50 transactions):

The ladder triples at each step except 6 → 5 characters (×5). Handles resell "in our token or usdc". Users choose a KYC path (credit card, custodial, taxed) or a non-KYC path (USDC on Solana or Ethereum). The plan budgets 100 staff at $200k, a $20m/yr burn.

The *Blockchain* note gives the philosophy: "ZK (privacy) & Low Storage (turn every device into a node)"; "Decentralization is foolhardy and can only be achieved through policy."; "Code isn't law. Law is Law."; "The tokens determinate the governance / Delegate tokens to foundation."

### Version 3: the dividend airdrop ($SUP, February 2023)

*$SUP - Support* sells ChatGPT bots trained on a protocol's docs ($500 total; $400 for the web pop-up, $100 more for Discord; 16 SOL or $400 USDC). Its token model is one Saheb had "been wanting to do sometime":

> [!quote] "We buy tokens off the market and then we airdrop it to our users. I.e the people that purchased the chat bots" — $SUP - Support, 2023-02-03

The token: "Create a Solana token, using initial funds that we raised from the team and divvy it up amongst ourselves. List it on Openbook." The company buys its own token on the market and gives it to customers, so buybacks double as loyalty rewards. Phase 3 is an opt-in Solana search ("This is how we win the Solana Hackathon for tools/infra"), widened because "Solana is too small in of itself for this to be a 10m project".

### Version 4: Saheb's rule of tokenomics (August–September 2023)

This page (2023-08-11, edited to 2023-09-10) is the closest thing to a constitution for the main coin. Its rules, verbatim:

1. "If you create an ecosystem coin, the ecosystem's coins must be less than the main coin." A $40 coin should swap into 10,000+ ecosystem coins so "they feel like the 10000 can then become invaluable as the first coin."
2. "Every product must have a token or face irrelevance." (Opensea vs Blur; Maestro vs Unibot.)
3. "Sound tokenomics are actually valid, look at OX, RLB, UNIBOT". Soundness is "revenue generation *any means necessary* and revenue sharing *any means necessary* buy and burn airdrop yield, many many more."
4. "21m supply is too little, nobody will buy the token if it's already 600" and "1b is too much. The main token needs to feel like a stock, stocks aren't priced at 9 zeros."
5. Price in the tens of dollars: "too liquidy feeling under 10, but below a thousand dollars". "Faith that your token can go from the tens of dollars to tens of thousands ala early ethereum is the ideal sweet spot."
6. "If you have 100m tokens at 40, and they go to 400, you have 40b." (META comparables imply 100x; Apple 1,000x.)
7. "Liquidity is king, give tokens freely of the side projects but make sure the attention is directed to the most liquid proper project."
8. "USERS UP = HOLD LET IT RIDE / USERS DOWN = SELL SELL SELL".
9. "Have revenue, but don't be defined by revenue."

It adds that memecoins are destined "to have 1-2 large" (DOGE and SHIB; PEPE and Bitcoin), and "Shoutout sisyphus, hes a mercenary but aren't we all."

> [!tension] The same page says ecosystem treasuries can be drained "back to the main holders and the main team through decisive decisions like cooler loans, or borrowing without repaying". That conflicts with the regulated path Saheb later takes with GLX and American Perpetual.

### Version 5: Coin Denominated Reality (November 2023)

*Coin Denominated Reality* (CDR), subtitled "Andromeda Fund Prospectus I" (2023-11-10, edited to 2025-06-25), opens: "Coin denominated reality means the way people, places, companies are denominated will be in coins." Its winner:

> [!quote] "a public company that holds a chain-main coin that holds in it's treasury ownership of various internet products coins which share revenue as dividends in usdc" — Coin Denominated Reality, 2023-11-10

The company pairs hardware and an OS that rivals iOS, Android, macOS and Windows with "hundred + revenue generating applications that have their ownership represented via coins." Verticals:

- **Proptech and social**: Airbnb, Turo and Omegle "x crypto"; video and livestream shopping with "a way to bet on all of it".
- **Fintech**: "Lend. Options. Perps."; "Crypto, stocks, and new corporations going public on main-chain coin"; multi-asset smart routing into stablecoins; prediction and idea markets; "bob's laundry service and frank's electrician company being bought and sold through one marketplace".

CDR predicts the death of email as the internet account, of the browser and of "transaction based state model blockchains", and says "all L1s are the wrong direction". Its macro call: a bull market to Q2 2025, then a ~30% correction in Q3 2025. A minimized copy (2024-02-13) adds nothing.

## Stable: Z, the prime stairsteps and the fiat rail (2023 → 2025) {#origins-stable}

Z is the only early token that uses the word "prime" in its design, and its stack names the first perpetual pair in Saheb's notes. It is backed by real estate, released in steps and defended by an in-house trading desk.

### How Z works (April 2023)

The *Stablecoin* note (created 2023-04-01, 02:34–02:56 UTC) calls Z "My overcollateralized stablecoin":

1. Building A is worth $60m. Borrow $10m against it.
2. The building backs "10m Z printing in a prime manner".
3. Lend the $10m to treasuries at 8%.
4. Pay property-NFT holders their rent in Z; keep the real-currency rent (e.g. AED).
5. "Z can be redeemed at market price for any currency at any time. CHF, AED, USD." That makes a Z forex market.

> [!number] 10m Z — printed against a $10m loan on a $60m building (Apr 2023)
> Loan proceeds earn 8% in treasuries; the peg is soft, "+-10% USD".

The peg is defended both ways:

- **Up**: "Z up protection, unlocks stair steps in the prime to release greater supply, because greater demand." "Rate of stair step release increases based on demand."
- **Down**: "Z down protection, rental income, arbitrage, trading is used to buy back Z to nominal ranges." A market-making team on Z/BTC defends "the soft peg of that 10% bid/ask spread".
- **Runway**: "Stairstep runway: how many prime stairsteps of runway we have available." Near zero, take another real-estate loan, lend at 8% and print more steps.

In the example the property earns $6m a year; 7m Z is outstanding and 3m sits in prime steps (about six months of runway); a falling price is met by buying back Z with the $3m earned.

> [!quote] "When people are only dumping, it's fine because you have over collateralized so much, your interest yield is higher than what you're paying to defend the peg. It's escape velocity." — Stablecoin, 2023-04-01

The stack: **Real Estate** → **Forex** (Z/USD, Z/EUR) → **Exchange** (Z/BTC, **BTCZPERP**, other pairs) → **Stable Coin** (Z) → **Portfolio Management** (the loan book). The note opens with a separate idea: companies install dishes as gateways, users pay for internet in crypto, and "they get yield in Z."

**What "prime stairsteps" means is never stated.** It could be prime-sized tranches, unlocks at prime-numbered steps, or a Fibonacci-prime schedule like the 2022 *fibonacci prime* note. The 2026 Rei/XR specification gives an explicit version: total supply is always a prime, and each step moves to the next prime and mints the gap ("mint = next prime − this prime"). Whether that was the 2023 meaning is unconfirmed.

> [!tension] Z is a redeemable stablecoin backed by real-estate loans. The note does not discuss money-transmission or securities treatment.

### StableStation (August 2025)

*StableStation* (2025-08-04) specs a Uniswap-style page that swaps USD and USDC both ways: "Fund your hyperliquid account in >2 minutes" (meaning under two). The flow: OAuth and Zero Hash KYC → Plaid bank link (an RTP-capable checking account; RTP is the instant US bank payment network) → a 60-second quote → deposit address or RTP/ACH instructions → webhooks → payout. "No Float Required: Zero Hash handles fiat custody and float / You never touch funds / You're just routing instructions + monitoring settlement".

> [!tension] The sample quote shows 100 USDC → $98.60 at "1 USDC = 0.986 USD" plus "Fee: $1.00". The rate alone gives $98.60, so the fee is inside it or counted twice; the spread is 1.4% either way.

A possible origin: "USDC App" and "USDC App Spec", owned by James Piazza III and shared with Saheb in July 2025, not read for this chapter.

## Funds: from Andromeda to 3S Capital and the "Prime bank" (2023 → 2024) {#origins-funds}

Andromeda is Saheb's plan to fund everything else with option trades: make a name with a signature call-spread strategy, become a regulated options market maker, grow into a prime bank, then build the consumer-tech company. The asks grow; the documents confirm no outside capital.

### Research Saheb received

In February 2024 Justin Drake (Ethereum Foundation) shared his based-preconfirmation working-group directory (about 393 people) and his "fixing fragmentation" notes: Ethereum L1 as the shared sequencer that orders rollup transactions, with preconfirmations (collateral-backed promises of inclusion), slashing for safety and liveness faults, and about 10% of proposers needed to opt in. Fragmentation is costed with Metcalfe's law: Arbitrum's $10B of $20B rollup TVL is a 4x loss; 1/50th of Ethereum's $500B is 2,500x. Saheb is not in the directory.
