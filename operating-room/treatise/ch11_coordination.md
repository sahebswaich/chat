## How the Operating Room runs it {#coord-room}

This is a recommendation, built from what the room already does.

- **One page per org, one owner per pod.** The home page draws the orgs around the core. Each pod
  lists its next moves; the coordination board sorts them into Now, Next and Later.
- **Decisions live in the treatise.** D1–D8 above are the register. When one is made, record it
  in the chapter that owns it and in its discussion thread, then update the pod's next moves.
- **Share by pod, not by folder.** Each audience gets a share link scoped to what it should see:
  MIAX to the AP chapters, a market maker to the street chapter, an investor to the deal chapter,
  a Rei backer to Chapter IX. Links can be read-only or allow comments, and can be turned off.
- **The desk trades from the terminal.** The terminal ranks setups by timeframe and sizes them to
  the funded account's rules; its journal records fills against the moves since.
- **Publish the outward documents from the room.** Briefs for MIAX, OCC, market makers and
  backers go out as publications on the room's domain, each with its own address.
