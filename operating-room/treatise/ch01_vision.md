# I. X → ∞ — one vision, many pods

Every crypto idea Saheb has written down since 2021 turns out to be one idea built several
times, each time closer to the real thing. This chapter states that idea, shows how the pieces
fit, and explains how the rest of the treatise is organized.

## The vision in one paragraph {#vision-paragraph}

**One account, one coin, one rail.** A single prime coin, **Rei (零), ticker XR**, whose supply
steps up through the prime numbers. A regulated rail, **American Perpetual**, that lists
perpetual futures on everything through MIAX Futures, clears at OCC, and pays its owner 60% of
the fees MIAX collects on every perpetual product. And around them, a set of pods: the desk that
trades it, the surfaces people use it through, and the capital and relationships that move it.
The variable changes from era to era (a currency, a fund, an exchange, a coin), but the shape
stays the same: one core asset that the whole system is denominated in, with everything else
built to make it worth more. That is what **X → ∞** means here.

> [!number] 60% — of the fees MIAX collects on every perpetual product, with no end date
> Master Perpetual Products License Agreement v2.8, final text of 30 September 2026.

> [!number] 2 → 3 → 5 → 7 — Rei's supply steps, one prime at a time
> 1 Rei = 10¹⁰ XR; supply starts at 2 Rei and each step mints the gap to the next prime, first split by Riemann's R function ("prime rata"). See Chapter IX.

*Sources: AP data room (30 Sep 2026); MPPLA v2.8 execution form; "Rei — The World a $100 Billion Coin Makes" (16 Oct 2026)*

## The shape that keeps coming back {#vision-shape}

Read in date order, the documents repeat three engines.

1. **A main coin that owns many product coins.** In August 2022 a single currency (OHM) prices
   the whole "World of Ohm". By October 2022 a fixed-supply main token sits above a family of
   app tokens in ManifestOS. In 2023 the rule is written down: ecosystem coins must stay below
   the main coin, and the main coin should be about 100 million tokens priced in the tens of
   dollars. *Coin Denominated Reality* (Nov 2023) puts a main chain coin in a public company's
   treasury above 100+ dividend-paying product coins. Rei is where this line lands.
2. **A fund that wants to become a bank.** Andromeda Capital Partners (2024) pools capital into
   options trades on ETH and SOL, then becomes ACM and the 3S Capital brochure, and names its
   end point as "the first legal crypto Prime bank in the world".
3. **An exchange where the house earns the fees.** BTCZPERP on the Z stablecoin (2023),
   "Lend. Options. Perps." (2023), the Andromeda Terminal (2024), then Greyline → GLX → American
   Perpetual (Nov 2025 → Sep 2026): perpetual futures on US stocks, built with MIAX, ending as a
   licence that pays 60% of every perpetual's fees.

The October 2026 Rei documents join all three: the coin is the main coin; MIAX's XR/USD book and
the American Perpetual stock perps sit in the same account; and the desk trades on top.

> [!quote] "If you have 100m tokens at 40, and they go to 400, you have 40b." — Saheb's rule of tokenomics, Aug 2023

*Sources: World of Ohm (Aug 2022); Master / ManifestOS (Oct 2022); Saheb's rule of tokenomics (Aug 2023); Coin Denominated Reality (Nov 2023); Andromeda Capital Partners (Feb 2024); GLX and AP documents (2025–26)*

## How the pods depend on each other {#vision-coordination}

The pods are not independent. Four links decide whether the whole system works.

- **The licence covers Rei unless it is carved out.** The MPPLA pays American Perpetual 60% of
  MIAX's fees on every perpetual product on any underlying. A Rei perpetual on MIAX would be one
  of them, and the bitcoin and ether perps are both American Perpetual's planned second class and
  Rei's planned first products at MIAX. The Rei × MIAX brief says to carve Rei out of the deal and
  its IP assignment before either is signed, and before the 10% sale.
- **The IP assignment sweeps in earlier work.** The September IP Assignment moves all
  pre-formation perpetual work into American Perpetual LLC. The June version kept five future
  ventures outside it (an FCM/broker-dealer, a trading app, white-label broker software, a
  DCM/DCO operating company and trading strategies). The desk and Rei both sit near that line.
- **One royalty, sold more than once on paper.** Silver Lake's June package bought 3.5 of 36
  royalty points; the September plan sells 10% of American Perpetual's units to one buyer after
  signing. Only one of these can be the live plan.
- **Relationships carry the launch.** John Smollen (MIAX, EVP New Product Development) is
  described in the GLX documents as the warm-introduction layer to Citadel, SIG, Jane Street,
  DRW and Jump; Joe Ferraro (President, MIAX Products) is named on the signing path. The market-maker program,
  the crypto class and the Rei book all run through the same people.

> [!tension] The MPPLA gives American Perpetual 60% of MIAX's fees on any perpetual product, and Rei's plan puts an XR market on MIAX: decide whether Rei is inside or outside the licence before signature.

## What this treatise is built from {#vision-sources}

The research read **about 240 documents and artifacts**: Saheb's Google Drive (the idea docs from 2021
on, the Greyline, GLX and Perps folders, the American Perpetual × MIAX data room in its
39-document "AI readable" edition, the closing set, the Silver Lake data room, the investor and
counsel documents) and his Claude workspace (the Rei, XR, Trader in Time, Hyperbull, AP data
room and other artifacts, plus the Claude Code sessions). Appendix C lists every source.

Three sources are not in yet, and the treatise says so wherever it matters:

- **iCloud Mail**, including the threads with John Smollen. This environment cannot reach iCloud;
  the importers in the repository pull it into Drive once it is connected (see `importers/`).
- **claude.ai and ChatGPT chats.** The Rei documents cite chats called "PRIME protocol" (25 May),
  "Prime token currency" (14 Jun) and "Private money system" (15 Jun), and there is a "Funded
  breakout coin leverage analysis" thread and a "zero-fee trenches" ChatGPT site. None of them
  are reachable from here; the harvest prompt and export importer bring them in.
- **iCloud Drive folders.** Those that are not mirrored to Google Drive.

Where documents disagree (and they often do, because the plan changed fast), the treatise gives
both values and flags the conflict in a **Tension** box.
